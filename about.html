ABOUT THE COMPANY

Curiosity first.
Craft follows.

Major Watch'up began with no inherited knowledge of how timepieces work, only a stubborn fascination with the way a small mechanism can hold a whole day together.

We learned by listening, sketching and taking things apart. Every collection is a fictional chapter from that curious workshop: Icelandic in spirit, precise in ambition and assembled one careful idea at a time.
