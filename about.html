ABOUT THE COMPANY

Three generations.
One craft.

JS Watch began in 2005 with a simple ambition: create timeless mechanical watches inspired by Iceland and assembled by hand in Reykjavik.

Ideas are developed and tested in our workshop before selected European components return to Iceland. Each watch is assembled, regulated and inspected by our own team.
