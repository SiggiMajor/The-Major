ABOUT THE COMPANY

Three generations.
One craft.

Founded in 2005 by four watch enthusiasts, JS Watch combines Icelandic design with traditional mechanical watchmaking. Ideas begin in Reykjavik, components are carefully selected in Europe, and every finished watch returns to the workshop for hand assembly, regulation and inspection.

Care for your timepiece

A few simple habits keep a mechanical watch accurate and ready for years to come.

Service

Mechanical watches benefit from a full service every 4–5 years. Cleaning, fresh oil, regulation and seal testing help protect the movement.

WATCH CARE →

Accuracy

Position, temperature and magnetism can affect the rate. A typical top-grade movement may perform within ±10 seconds per day.

READ THE GUIDE →

Water resistance

Ratings describe controlled pressure, not diving depth. Keep the crown closed and remember that water can shorten the life of a leather strap.

Can I visit the workshop?

Yes. The workshop and showroom are at Laugavegur 62 in central Reykjavik. Opening hours are Monday–Friday 10:00–18:00 and Saturday 10:00–16:00.

Can I buy from outside Iceland?

Yes. International customers can shop online or contact the team directly for help with a purchase, current prices and delivery options.

Can I arrange a personal appointment?

Yes. If you cannot visit Reykjavik, a personal FaceTime appointment can be booked with a JS Watch expert.

How long does a mechanical watch run?

A typical automatic movement can hold around 38–42 hours of power reserve when fully wound. Wearing it regularly helps the movement stay running.

What is covered by the guarantee?

JS Watch describes a two-year guarantee against defects in materials and workmanship. Unapproved opening or improper handling may affect coverage.
