FROM IDEA TO WRIST

A tiny machine.

How it works

We build timepieces in small numbers, with curiosity, patience and personal attention at every stage.

01 / STORY

Inspired by place

Every Major Watch'up begins with an Icelandic mood: weather, water, light, maps or a story worth carrying.

02 / DESIGN

Drawn in Reykjavik

Proportions, dials and details are refined through sketches and patient experiments before anything is assembled.

03 / ASSEMBLY

Built by hand

Selected mechanical components meet in the workshop, where each movement is fitted, regulated and checked.

04 / JOURNEY

Prepared for life

Each order is inspected, packed and sent with care, ready to become part of someone else's story.

What makes it different

Not mass production. Not disposable time. A small workshop with a point of view.

Made by hand in Reykjavik

Every watch is assembled, regulated and inspected in our own workshop before it leaves for a new owner.

Designed for a lifetime

With regular care, a mechanical watch can stay useful for decades and become something worth passing on.

Personal service

Questions, straps, sizing and repairs are handled by a small team that knows the work behind each timepiece.

Small numbers
